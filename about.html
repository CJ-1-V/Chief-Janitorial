Our story

We go beyond basic cleaning

Chief Janitorial was founded in Charlottetown with a simple idea: businesses deserve a cleaning partner that communicates, shows up consistently and takes pride in the details.

Today we serve corporate offices, healthcare facilities, retail stores, food plants and industrial warehouses across Prince Edward Island. We excel in communication, training and record keeping — ensuring your needs are met every clean.
