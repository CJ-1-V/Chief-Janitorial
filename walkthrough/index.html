Who it's for

  • Food processing plants across PEI: seafood and shellfish, potato and vegetable, meat, dairy, bakery and packers
  • Plant managers, QA and food safety leads, and operations managers
  • Plants working to CFIA, HACCP and GMP expectations
  • Also available for property managers and multi-site landlords who want a walkthrough of their buildings (see "Not a food plant?" below)

What the free walkthrough includes

  1. 1

    Walk the floor together. We meet your plant or QA lead and walk your production and support areas.

  2. 2

    Look at your sanitation schedule. High-risk zones, changeover points, and hard-to-reach areas such as drains, overheads and under equipment.

  3. 3

    Review your records. How sanitation is recorded today, and whether it would hold up to an auditor.

  4. 4

    Share quick wins. We tell you what we noticed, with no obligation.

It's a conversation, not a sales pitch. If Chief Janitorial is a fit for your sanitation needs, great. If not, you still walk away with useful observations.

How long it takes

About 60 to 90 minutes on site, depending on the size of your plant.

We schedule around your production, including before or after shifts.

  • A short written summary of what we saw and the quick wins we suggest
  • If you want it, a proposed sanitation scope and a free quote

FAQ

Is the walkthrough really free?

Yes. There's no cost and no obligation.

Will this disrupt production?

No. We work around your schedule and follow your plant's GMP, PPE and visitor rules on site.

Do you need to see our documents?

Only what you're comfortable sharing. Looking at how sanitation is recorded today helps us give useful feedback. We treat everything we see as confidential.

Is this a CFIA inspection or a certified audit?

No. It's an informal walkthrough by a sanitation contractor, not a CFIA inspection, third-party audit or certification.

Which areas of PEI do you cover?

Charlottetown, Stratford and Cornwall, plus commercial and food plant sites across PEI.

Not a food plant?

We also do walkthroughs for property managers, landlords, offices, clinics and retail sites. Choose your facility type in the form below.

What happens after the walkthrough?

You get a short summary of our observations. If you'd like, we'll prepare a sanitation scope and a free quote.
